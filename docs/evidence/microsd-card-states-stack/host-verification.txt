== Making the card blank
`diskutil zeroDisk` was started; it sat idle (no card I/O, iostat 0.00 MB/s) for 53 minutes and was killed,
after which diskutil hung until the reader was re-plugged. How much was zeroed is unknown. After the re-plug:
  Device / Media Name: USB3.0 CRW   -SD ; Disk Size 15931539456 B ; File System: None ; Content (IOContent): None
  diskutil list: no partition map, no volume (macOS offered "The disk you attached was not readable").
That is the "blank" state used below: no partition table and no FAT volume. Bytes beyond the first sectors may
still hold the old data; the content of the rest of the card was not read.
