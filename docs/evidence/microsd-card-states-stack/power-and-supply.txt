Hub switching and supply readings of the 3 November 2026 runs.
Transcribed from the terminal output of the session; no raw capture file exists for them.
Commands: uhubctl -l 0-1.4 -p 4 -a on   (before the wrong-layout and stack runs; for the blank run the port was already
                                         powered, no -a on was run, and the DAPLink showed on port 4)
          uhubctl -l 0-1.4 -p 4 -a off -f (after each run, after the restore image was flashed)
          JLinkExe -device MIMXRT1062xxx6A -if SWD -speed 400 -NoGui 1 -ExitOnError 0 -CommanderScript vtref.jlink   (hwinfo; reads VTref)

After "-a on":  Port 4: 0103 power enable connect [0d28:0204 ARM DAPLink CMSIS-DAP ...]
After "-a off": Port 4: 0000 off   (then 0080 off, 0000 off)

Runs and the supply read after the final off (J-Link-read VTref):
  blank card         VTref=0.000V
  wrong layout       VTref=0.000V
  stack probe        VTref=0.000V
VTref before each run (preflight logs): 3.322 V.

Hub topology at the start of the blank run (uhubctl): hub 0-1.4 port 4 held the DAPLink; the J-Link was on hub 0-1 port 1.
No scripted power cut was part of these runs: the hub switch was used only to power the board on (two runs) and off (after each run). In the card-removal attempts the operator unplugged the power cable by hand (see the README).

Card-removal run (12:37-12:56 UTC): uhubctl -l 0-1.4 -p 4 -a on before the run; port 4 showed the DAPLink within 6 s;
VTref 3.322 V in the preflight; after the restore image was flashed: uhubctl -l 0-1.4 -p 4 -a off -f, Port 4: 0000 off,
VTref=0.000V. In attempt 2 the operator unplugged and re-plugged the board's power cable by hand (not a hub
operation); that reset the board.
